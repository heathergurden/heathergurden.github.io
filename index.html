Background

A Curious Mind. A People-First Perspective.

I’ve always wanted to understand how things work, why they work that way, and how they could work better. That curiosity took me from recruiting to software training to leading technology projects and AI initiatives, and it still shapes how I approach every challenge.

I enjoy connecting dots and making complicated things feel approachable, whether that’s a quick reference guide for a busy attorney or a training program for hundreds of employees. My background encompasses leading large-scale M365 and SharePoint migrations, authoring user adoption documentation, and applying rhetorical frameworks to technological workflows.

Outside of work, family, faith, and community are at the center of my life. You’ll find me mountain biking, snowboarding, working out, cooking, reading, or chasing the next thing I want to learn.
