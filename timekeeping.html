At a Glance

Problem

A law firm rolling out a timekeep software needed attorneys and staff to record time correctly from day one, with little time to read a manual.

Approach

I learned the system ahead of rollout and wrote task-based guides for the jobs users needed first: adding a time entry, working on behalf of another timekeeper, and using temporary matter codes.

Result

Short, searchable guides on the firm’s intranet that let timekeepers find an answer, act, and get back to billable work. Sensitive details have been redacted.

Design Rationale

Key Decisions

01

One question per guide

Busy professionals want a direct answer to the question in front of them, not a manual. Each guide answers a single task with numbered steps and imperative language, so users can scan, act, and move on.

02

Flagging the mistakes that reach client invoices

Errors like the decimal hour conversion (1.8 = 1 hour 48 minutes), narrative billing rules, and the monthly release deadline carry through to Billing. I pulled them out of the steps into NOTE callouts so they couldn’t be missed.

03

Publishing where people already look

Timekeepers find help one task at a time, usually through search, so I published the guides on the firm’s intranet as searchable resources. I used real screenshots and redacted client and personnel details to keep them accurate and confidential.
